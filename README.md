## What is Modelify?

Modelify is an AI product photography platform built specifically for fashion e-commerce.

It empowers apparel brands, Shopify merchants, and marketplace sellers to transform clothing product photos into high-converting on-model photography for online stores, lookbooks, ad campaigns, and social media.

### Core Capabilities
- 👗 **Generate on-model fashion imagery** from flat-lays, ghost mannequins, or hanger photos
- 🌐 **Diverse global model roster** across multiple demographics, ages, and body shapes
- 📐 **Automated 4-angle consistent generation** (Front, 45°, Side, Back)
- 💰 **Reduce photoshoot costs by over 95%** with instant delivery
- 🚀 **Accelerate drop schedules** from weeks to minutes

🌐 **Try Modelify Today:** [https://modelify.fit](https://modelify.fit)

## How Modelify Works

```text
Product Image (Flat-lay / Mannequin)
               │
               ▼
Upload to Modelify & Auto-Preprocess
               │
               ▼
Select Model Persona, Style & Environment
               │
               ▼
AI Generation (4-Angle Consistent Engine)
               │
               ▼
Commercial-Ready Fashion Imagery (WebP/Shopify)
```
