# Fashion Prompt Library

[English](prompt-library.md) · [简体中文](prompt-library.zh-CN.md)

These prompts are starting points for art direction. Keep prompts concise and avoid asking for changes that conflict with the actual garment.

## Clean e-commerce studio

> Professional fashion e-commerce photography, clean studio background, natural standing pose, balanced soft lighting, realistic fabric texture, clear garment silhouette, commercial catalog style.

## Premium editorial

> Premium fashion editorial photography, refined studio lighting, confident natural pose, subtle luxury aesthetic, realistic skin and fabric texture, clean composition, garment remains the visual focus.

## Minimal lifestyle

> Modern minimal lifestyle fashion photo, softly lit neutral interior, relaxed natural pose, realistic proportions, understated styling, product clearly visible, suitable for an online fashion store.

## Streetwear

> Contemporary streetwear campaign photography, urban setting, confident relaxed pose, natural daylight, modern commercial styling, realistic garment drape, product details remain clear.

## Activewear

> Commercial activewear photography, athletic natural pose, bright clean environment, realistic body proportions, crisp fabric detail, energetic but uncluttered composition.

## Resort / swim

> Premium resort fashion photography, soft natural daylight, clean upscale setting, relaxed confident pose, realistic skin tones and fabric texture, elegant commercial composition.

## Front-facing catalog

> Front-facing full-body catalog image, neutral natural stance, even studio lighting, clean background, garment fully visible from neckline to hem, minimal accessories.

## Three-quarter view

> Three-quarter fashion catalog view, natural posture, clean studio environment, soft balanced lighting, garment shape and construction clearly visible.

## Detail emphasis

> Commercial fashion photo with emphasis on garment construction and fabric texture, clean lighting, natural pose, minimal styling distractions, accurate color and realistic material detail.

## Prompting principles

### Do

- Describe the photography style
- Describe lighting
- Describe pose in simple terms
- Describe the environment
- Keep the garment as the main subject
- Ask for realistic proportions and texture

### Avoid

- Asking for a different garment than the uploaded product
- Overloading the prompt with conflicting styles
- Excessive accessories that may cover the product
- Very extreme poses when product fidelity matters
- Unnecessary camera jargon unless it serves a clear goal

## Useful formula

A practical prompt can follow:

```text
[photography type]
+ [environment]
+ [pose]
+ [lighting]
+ [commercial goal]
+ [fidelity requirement]
```

Example:

> Premium e-commerce fashion photography, neutral studio background, relaxed standing pose, soft diffused lighting, clean catalog composition, realistic fabric texture and accurate garment silhouette.
